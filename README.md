# Stat 1080 - Data and the Stories They Tell

# Textbook

The textbooks used for this class are free and available online.
If you would like to purchase a print copy of the Intro to Data Science textbook 
which includes volume 1 and 2, you can get the 
[1st edition from CRC for <$70](https://www.routledge.com/Introduction-to-Data-Science-Data-Wrangling-and-Visualization-with-R/Irizarry/p/book/9781032116556) 
or you can get [a PDF copy from LeanPub and choose your price (suggested $50, minimum $0)](https://leanpub.com/datasciencebook). 

-   [Introduction to Data Science 1, by Rafael A. Irizarry](https://rafalab.dfci.harvard.edu/dsbook-part-1/)
-   [Introduction to Data Science 2, by Rafael A. Irizarry](https://rafalab.dfci.harvard.edu/dsbook-part-2/)

In addition, I will occasionally assign readings from another online textbook that I wrote, `Statistical Computing in R and Python`, even though this course is taught entirely in R. 
Unfortunately, this one is not available in print (yet), but you can get it online. 
I recommend reading this textbook with an eye to concepts, because working in two languages simultaneously almost requires that you focus on the concepts that are common between the two languages -- that is, the underlying data science concepts that are consistent across different workflows and implementations. 

-   [Statistical Computing in R and Python](https://srvanderplas.github.io/stat-computing-r-python). 

I am in the process of editing this book and have a contract with Wiley to publish it in late 2027.
